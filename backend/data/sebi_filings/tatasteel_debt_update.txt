[DOCUMENT_ID: SEBI-REG30-TATASTEEL-2025Q3-02]
COMPANY: Tata Steel Limited (NSE: TATASTEEL)
FILING_TYPE: SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 - Regulatory & Debt Disclosure
DATE: 2025-11-04
SECTION: Global Restructuring, UK Grant & Debt Reduction Progress

1. Financial Summary:
Consolidated revenue for the quarter reached INR 54,800 Crores with Indian operations delivering an industry-leading EBITDA per tonne of INR 15,200. Crude steel production in India stood at 5.27 million tonnes (+4.8% YoY).

2. UK Operations & Transition to Green Steel:
Under the agreed restructuring framework with the UK Government, the £500 million grant grant-in-aid agreement has been formalized for the 3.2 MTPA Electric Arc Furnace (EAF) at Port Talbot. Legacy blast furnaces have been safely decommissioned. Restructuring costs of INR 3,100 Crores have been fully provisioned in exceptional items, eliminating future operational cash burn in the UK.

3. Netherlands Blast Furnace & Decarbonization:
Ijmuiden plant operated at 91% capacity utilization with stable spreads. Discussions with the Dutch Government on subsidy support for clean steel infrastructure are progressing constructively.

4. Net Debt & Deleveraging Trajectory:
Net debt reduced by INR 4,250 Crores during the half-year to INR 78,500 Crores. Group liquidity remains robust at INR 16,800 Crores. Net Debt / EBITDA stands at 2.45x, on track towards target of below 2.0x.

5. Risk Factors & Market Outlook:
Global steel prices faced margin pressure from Chinese export dumping; domestic infrastructure demand bolstered volumes by 11% YoY. Raw material costs (coking coal) softened by $24/tonne QoQ.
