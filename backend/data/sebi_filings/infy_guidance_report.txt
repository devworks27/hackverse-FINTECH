COMPANY: Infosys Limited (NSE: INFY)
FILING_TYPE: SEBI (LODR) Regulations, 2015 - Guidance & Earnings Release
DATE: 2025-10-17
SECTION: Revenue Guidance Revision & Topaz AI Pipeline

1. Financial Performance:
Quarterly revenue grew to INR 41,200 Crores (+5.8% YoY). Operating margin stood at 21.2%. Free cash flow generation reached $840 Million.

2. FY26 Constant Currency Guidance:
Revised FY26 annual revenue growth guidance upward to 4.75% - 5.5% (from earlier 3.75% - 4.5%), citing strong recovery in North American financial services client discretionary spending.

3. Infosys Topaz & Cobalt Engagements:
Over 220 generative AI projects in active pipeline. Average deal size for AI-first transformational contracts grew by 35% QoQ.

4. Headcount & Attrition:
Voluntary trailing 12-month attrition declined to 12.9%. Added 3,500 net freshers in global delivery centers.
